## 📖 About

The Legend of Heroes: Trails in the Sky 2nd Chapter by Nihon Falcom (2006, Western release 2015) concludes the Liberl arc of the Trails series. Following the cliffhanger of the first game, Estelle and Joshua face the Ouroboros organization in a turn-based RPG celebrated for its intricate world-building and cast of hundreds.
